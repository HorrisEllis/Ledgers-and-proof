# Ledgers and proof

Private. The record that must not be lost: ledgers, ideas, proof of competency, and the specs that hold today's decisions.

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `ledgers/` | Session ledgers (what was done, where, the proof), the Evidence Ledger, the autofill gap ledger, Idearium's phase ledger |
| `ideas/` | The Idea Hub, his words mapped (Eravos HIS-WORDS-MAP), raw notes as he wrote them |
| `proof/` | Proof-of-competency records (Nexus, Eravos) and the Eravos competence register |
| `specs/` | Copies of the specs that carry the decisions: genesis, architecture, the Idea Hub phasemap, the mind domain |

Rules:
- Append, never rewrite. A correction is a new entry; the original stays.
- His words are quoted exactly, with the date.
- The source of truth for a spec stays in its own repo; the copy here is the snapshot of the day.
