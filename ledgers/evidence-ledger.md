# Evidence Ledger

Nov 14, 2026 · @James

## How to use it

One entry each time a first read fires and you check it before acting. The point isn't to stop the trigger. It's to log the gap between the first read and what you did.

Copy this for each entry:

- **When:**
- **What happened:** the words or event, exactly as they were
- **First read:** what my brain said it meant
- **What I almost did:**
- **What I checked:** reread it, looked for another meaning, asked
- **What it actually was:**
- **What I did instead:**
- **Evidence:** what happened after

## Entries

### Nov 14, 8:45pm

- **What happened:** Chloe said it's "so nice to just sit like this without them complaining", about being with animals.
- **First read:** she's talking about me. She's saying I'm negative and complain all the time.
- **What I almost did:** typed that I could see why it seemed that way, and that I'd be more lighthearted.
- **What I checked:** kept looking and read it again.
- **What it actually was:** she meant animals, and the quiet of being with them without expectation.
- **What I did instead:** answered what she actually said, about silence without expectation.
- **Evidence:** she shared something personal and I met it instead of defending myself. First time I've caught it before sending.
